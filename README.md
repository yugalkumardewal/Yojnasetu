# 🚀 YojnaSetu (योजनासेतु)
### *Bridging the Gap Between Business Dreams and Government Schemes*

**YojnaSetu** is an AI-powered platform designed to empower aspiring entrepreneurs to create their own business empires. The platform addresses a critical socio-economic challenge: the massive information gap between complex government schemes and the people who need them most. 

By aggregating live data from official government portals, YojnaSetu ensures that every user—whether **literate or illiterate**—can easily discover and access financial aid, subsidies, and growth opportunities.

---

## ✨ Key Features

*   **🤖 AI-Driven Scheme Matching:** Analyzes a user's business idea and automatically matches it with the most relevant active government schemes.
*   **🗣️ Voice-First Accessibility:** Built-in Speech-to-Text and Text-to-Speech capabilities, allowing illiterate or visually impaired users to navigate the platform entirely via voice commands.
*   **🌐 Multi-Language Support:** Breaks language barriers by translating complex policy jargon into simple, local regional languages.
*   **🔄 Live Govt Data Sync:** Automatically fetches and updates scheme details directly from official government portals to prevent outdated information.
